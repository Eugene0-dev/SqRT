# SqRT

### Square RunTime

A geometric tower defense game with a little bit of chaos, questionable management decisions, and an unreasonable amount of triangles.

## About

In SqRT, you protect a Square from waves of hostile Circles.

Fortunately, you're not alone. A Triangle is here to help — an autonomous defender that moves, targets enemies, charges its weapon, and fires powerful laser blasts.

But survival is only the beginning.

Defeated enemies leave behind numerical resources. Collect them, grow your numbers, and gradually turn a desperate defense into an increasingly absurd geometric economy.

## Features

- **Geometric combat** — Squares, Circles, and Triangles, each with their own role.
- **Autonomous defense** — your Triangle handles targeting, movement, and combat on its own.
- **Laser weapons** — charge, aim, and blast incoming enemies.
- **Incremental mechanics** — collect numerical resources and build towards something bigger.
- **Procedural visuals** — shaders, animated effects, and a universe made without external art assets.

## Development Status

SqRT is an early-stage experimental project.

Core gameplay systems are under active development. Features, balance, visuals, and mechanics may change significantly.

## Built With

- [Godot Engine](https://godotengine.org/)
